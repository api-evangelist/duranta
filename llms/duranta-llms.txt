# Duranta Labs

> Duranta Labs is an applied AI laboratory building geospatial and computer-vision technology to understand the physical world. Its foundation vision model, Aidan, generates accurate, current 4D digital twins from optical imagery. Duranta is a proptech company backed by Fifth Wall. As of this writing Duranta publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDK — this file summarizes the company's public surface for agents.
